The complaint

Crews say Nabd is slow, and everyone blames the model

  • A request runs four kinds of step: retrieval, a model call, a tool call, a second model call.
  • Logs say the request took long. They do not say which step.
  • A trace records every step of one request as a span, with its parent, its duration and its attributes.

Guessing costs

Swapping to a faster model would have cost a release, an evaluation and a gate, and fixed nothing.

Lab 3.1 · steps 1 and 2

A provider, a processor, an exporter, then a span around each step

exporter = InMemorySpanExporter()           # production: OTLP to a collector
provider = TracerProvider()
provider.add_span_processor(SimpleSpanProcessor(exporter))
tracer = provider.get_tracer("wahaj.nabd")

class TracedNabd(nabd.Nabd):
    def retrieve(self, question):
        with tracer.start_as_current_span("retrieval manuals") as span:
            span.set_attribute("gen_ai.operation.name", "retrieval")
            return super().retrieve(question)

In production a BatchSpanProcessor and an OTLP exporter send spans to a collector; Jaeger, Grafana Tempo, Langfuse and Phoenix accept OTLP. The lab keeps spans in memory so nothing leaves the laptop and no account is needed.

A drift statistic

The population stability index compares a day's distribution with a reference

edges = quantiles(reference, deciles)
ref = share of reference per bin
cur = share of today per bin
psi = sum((cur - ref) * ln(cur / ref))
PSIRule of thumb
below 0.1stable
0.1 to 0.25a moderate move
above 0.25a large move

PSI is one choice; the Kolmogorov-Smirnov statistic and the Wasserstein distance are others. The rules of thumb come from credit scoring and are starting points, not laws: Session 2's lab tunes them on a replayed month.

Lab 3.2 · step 3

Each tuning rule removes one kind of false alarm

Rolling reference

The previous seven days: the season is absorbed, a step is not.

Minimum sample

Fewer than 200 feeders: not judged.

Persistence

Above 0.2 on three judged days in a row.

51 → 2
alerts: load from day 12 (fired day 14), dust from day 22 (fired day 24)

Persistence costs two days of delay. Ask: is two days acceptable for a classifier scored daily and labelled 30 days later? For a fraud model it might not be.

Drift or skew

Load stepped up about 10% overnight while temperatures fell: the meters changed, not the grid

Skew

Serving computes a feature differently from training. Here: recalibrated meters read high from day 12. Fix the data; retraining would learn the error.

Drift

The world changes. Here: dust rises from day 21. Watch the labels and consider retraining when they arrive.

Stretch: the median ratio of serving load to the same feeder's batch load steps by about 10% on day 12.

The ratio is about 0.93 on days 9 to 11 and about 1.03 from day 12. Who to call: the SCADA export team, the same owner as Day 1's data contract.

What the tuned monitor cannot see

A rolling reference absorbs a slow drift, so keep one fixed comparison too

  • Daily: rolling seven-day reference, for steps and fast changes.
  • Monthly: against the training snapshot, for slow moves that matter.
  • When labels arrive: the model's own metrics by region, which inputs cannot replace.
Three files leave the lab. The dashboard (PSI per feature per day, with the threshold and the alerts), the alert rules, and the note: shifts found, alarms removed and by which rule, what is no longer visible. Lamya signs the note.

Inputs can move without hurting the model, and the model can fail with stable inputs. Session 4 turns the alerts into a retraining decision. The lab draws the dashboard as one image and writes the rules as a JSON file, so both run anywhere; in production the same series go to Prometheus and Grafana, or to a tool such as Evidently, and the rules become alert rules there.

Routing

Every failure and one pass in ten go to the reviewers' queue

32
traces for review from a sample of 100
  • Failures: confirm or overturn; each overturn is a calibration label.
  • Random passes: what the grader lets through.
  • Each review returns to the calibration set, so the grader's numbers stay current.

Saqer's reviewers have a fixed hour a day: size the sample so the queue fits the hour. The queue CSV holds the redacted question, never the original.

Stretch · retention

Keep traces for a stated time, longer only while a reviewer needs them

  • Wahaj's rule in the lab: redacted traces kept 3 days for debugging; those in the review queue held until reviewed.
  • On day 7: 221 traces deleted, 179 kept, 32 of them held for review.
  • PDPL Article 18: personal data is destroyed once it is no longer needed for its purpose.

The three-day period is the course's assumption, not a legal figure: the data protection officer (Nouf Al-Sadhan in the case) sets it with the system owner.

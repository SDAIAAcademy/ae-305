Format

8 minutes for each defence, questions included

1 · The release log · 2 min

Each decision of the week, the metric it was taken on, the evidence file.

2 · The injected fault · 5 min

Find it from traces and logs, say what you would do and who decides, do the first step.

3 · The runbook · 1 min

What this week would change in it.

Peer feedback. After each defence, everyone writes one strength, one question and one suggestion.

Inject the fault into a copy of the participant's setup, never into their repository. Pick from the assessor's list, and vary it across participants who present one after another. The participant diagnoses without an assistant during the defence.

# MM-02 · Ring main unit switching

## 1. Before switching

Check the gas pressure gauge of the ring main unit. A gauge in the red zone means no switching: report it to the control room and wait for a specialist.

## 2. Switching sequence

1. Confirm the switching programme number. 2. Check the gas pressure. 3. On R-24 units, check the earth switch interlock is engaged. 4. Operate the load-break switch with the insulated handle. 5. Confirm the indicator shows open. 6. Report the new state to the control room.

## 3. Faults during switching

If the switch does not move, stop. Do not force the handle. Report the unit number.

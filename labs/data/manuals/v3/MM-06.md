# MM-06 · Vegetation clearance

## 1. Clearance distances

Keep trees at least 3 metres from 13.8 kV conductors and 4 metres from 33 kV conductors.

## 2. Palm trees

Palm fronds are cut back to 4 metres from any conductor, whatever the voltage.

## 3. Landowners

Inform the landowner before cutting on private land, through the customer-care team.

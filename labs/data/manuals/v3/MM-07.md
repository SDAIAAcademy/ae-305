# MM-07 · Heat stress and work-rest

## 1. Heat index

Above a heat index of 46 degrees, outdoor work follows a cycle of 40 minutes of work and 20 of rest.

## 2. Water

Each crew member drinks at least 250 ml of water every 20 minutes in summer.

## 3. Signs

Stop work and cool the person at once on confusion, headache or no sweating; call the duty medic.

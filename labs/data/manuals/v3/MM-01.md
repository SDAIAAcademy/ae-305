# MM-01 · Isolation and earthing before work

## 1. Scope

This manual applies to every crew working on Wahaj medium-voltage feeders and ring main units. No work starts on a conductor until the isolation is complete and recorded.

## 2. Isolation steps

1. Obtain the switching programme from the control room. 2. Open the feeder breaker and the load-break switch. 3. Lock and tag every isolation point with the crew lock. 4. Prove dead with an approved voltage tester, tested before and after use. 5. Apply earths on both sides of the work point. 6. Record the permit number with the control room.

## 3. Returning to service

Remove earths only after every crew member has signed off the permit. The control room closes the breaker; a crew never re-energises a feeder.

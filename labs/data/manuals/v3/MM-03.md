# MM-03 · Transformer oil sampling

## 1. When to sample

Sample distribution transformers above 500 kVA every twelve months and after any fault trip.

## 2. Sampling steps

Use a clean glass bottle, flush the valve with one litre, fill to the shoulder, and label the bottle with the transformer number and the date.

## 3. Results

A dielectric strength below 30 kV in the laboratory result means the oil must be treated or replaced.
